Norsk Cards — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 23, 2026

Norsk Cards is a Norwegian vocabulary flashcard app.

Data collection

Norsk Cards does not collect any data. Your study progress and settings are stored only on your device. The app makes no network requests and has no accounts, analytics, or tracking.

Subscriptions

The optional Norsk Pro subscription is processed entirely by Apple through your Apple ID. We never see your payment details. Manage or cancel it in Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions.
